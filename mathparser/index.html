Mathparser

Supported syntax

+ −
Addition and subtraction, including a leading sign, e.g. -5
* /
Multiplication and division
%
Modulo (remainder)
^
Power, e.g. 2^10
( )
Grouping, can be nested

Decimals and spaces are fine; spaces are ignored. Up to 1024 characters.

Press Enter or click Calculate to evaluate.

Division by zero, unbalanced brackets and empty input are reported as errors, not crashes.

Expression calculator

Enter a math expression and press Calculate.
2 + 2 * 2 - 1/0.5 + 3^2 + (7 + 7) * 0.1 evaluates to 14.4.

Pure JavaScript · no frameworks, no build step
